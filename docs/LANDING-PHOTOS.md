## The big-organization prompts (now on the page)
1. energy: At the switch-on of a new solar farm at dusk, a utility CEO in a hard hat and a crew of linemen laugh and
   hug as rows of panels and a substation glow behind them; one engineer covers her mouth in disbelief.
2. manufacturing: The ribbon-cutting night of a vast new auto plant: executives in suits and line workers in
   coveralls cheer together on the factory floor as the first car rolls off the line under flash.
3. mining: At the mouth of a reopened lithium mine at night, the mine manager and a crew with headlamps spray
   champagne beside a haul truck, red dirt glittering in the flash.
4. investments: Late night in a glass boardroom, a foreign investor and an American mayor shake hands and laugh over
   signed papers, a city skyline wet with rain behind them, staff clapping.
5. robotics: In a new robotics plant before dawn, an engineer team in black jackets laughs as a robot arm hands its
   first finished part to the plant's founder, sparks and work lights in the background.
6. computing centers: Outside a newly powered data center in the rain, a construction crew and the operator's
   executives cheer under the flash as rows of server halls light up behind the fence.

Variety rule (founder, 2026-11-01): no two slides share a prompt shape. Not every scene is a gray-haired man laughing
with his head back: vary age, gender, background, mood (quiet, tired, tearful, wild), time of day and framing.
What reads as real (2026-11-01): prompt it as a cheap point-and-shoot flash snapshot, crooked framing, slightly out
of focus, overexposed faces, red-eye, someone half cut off, a blurry hand in front, ordinary unflattering faces, mud.
Polished, centered, symmetrical, eyes-closed or model-pretty scenes read as AI.

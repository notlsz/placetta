# Sales playbook: quoting Placetta

One page for a new hire. Prices are in `src/pricing.ts` and `docs/PRICING.md`; the research behind every comparison is
in `docs/PRICING-RESEARCH.md`. Never quote a number that isn't on this page.

## The prices, from memory

**$149 a store a month, billed yearly. $299 a new license. $999 a complex one. $99 a renewal the plan doesn't cover.
The plan preview is free. Government fees at cost.**

- The **store plan** ($1,788 a store a year) covers tracking every license and renewal date, renewal monitoring,
  rule-change alerts, the document vault, Odelia for the whole team, and every renewal Odelia files on her own.
- **10 or more stores on the plan:** every new license is included too. This is the plan to lead with for chains.

## How to quote

1. **Start with the free plan preview.** Ask for the business type and the city, and have the prospect make the plan
   (about a minute). It lists every license, its agency, the agency's fee and the timeline. Quote from that list, not
   from memory.
2. **Count the new licenses.** Each is $299. Each one on the complex list is $999. Anything staff earn themselves
   (food handler cards, food manager certificates) and inspections inside a permit are tracked free.
3. **Add the store plan:** $1,788 a store a year.
4. **Say the government fees separately,** from the plan, at cost. Never fold them into Placetta's price.

Worked example (Placetta's own plan for a restaurant in San José): 9 licenses × $299 + 2 building plan reviews × $999
= **$4,689 to open**, plus **$1,788** for the first year of the store plan, plus agency fees at cost.

## The complex list (fixed; nothing else is $999)

1. **Liquor licenses**: an alcoholic beverage license, a beer and wine license, a conditional use permit to sell alcohol.
2. **Building plan review**: a building permit for a tenant improvement or build-out, a food facility plan check or
   plan review.
3. **Import permits**: an import permit or import license from an agency.

If you think something else is hard, it's still $299. Raise it with the founder; don't quote a different price.

## Renewals

- On the store plan, every renewal Odelia files on her own is included.
- A renewal that needs our team (a wet signature, mail only, an in-person visit) or for a store without the plan is
  $99. Odelia starts it 60 days before it's due; the owner approves it with one tap and it's charged then, never before.

## When to offer the store plan for chains

- **10 or more stores:** always lead with it. Every store at $149 a month, every new license included. Show the math:
  three restaurant openings a year are about $14,000 in new licenses at list price, all included.
- **2 to 9 stores:** the store plan for every store, new licenses at $299 and $999. Tell them that at 10 stores every
  new license is included.
- **Franchisors:** each franchisee usually pays for its own stores; headquarters sees every store.

## Rules

- **No discounts.** No promotion codes, no multi-year discounts, no "first license free". If a prospect needs a
  different deal, the founder decides, in writing.
- **Agency fees at cost.** Never mark them up, never estimate one the agency doesn't publish; say "the agency sets it,
  and Odelia confirms it before you pay".
- **Every charge is shown before approval** and nothing is charged without it. Say this in every call.
- **No promises about dates or outcomes.** Timelines are estimates. Don't offer an open-by-date guarantee or a lapse
  guarantee: neither is part of the store plan.
- **Customers from before October 2026 keep their plans.** Don't move them; point them to Settings, Billing.

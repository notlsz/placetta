## Cost and time to open (US)
- Institute for Justice, 20 cities: 45 steps, 8 agencies; restaurant permit fees avg >$4,900, SF ~$22,600 (https://ij.org/press-release/new-report-reveals-how-cities-make-it-needlessly-expensive-time-consuming-and-challenging-to-start-a-small-business/).
- Fairfax County VA 2025, 34 restaurants: median 315 days to permit; median fees $6,234; owners estimate $70k–$150k lost per week of delay (unverified by county) (https://www.fairfaxcounty.gov/boardofsupervisors/sites/boardofsupervisors/files/Assets/documents/Evaluating-the-Restaurant-Permitting-Process-Study-Draft.pdf).
- Expediters (vendor figures): restaurant $2,000–$7,500 over 6–12 weeks; multi-site $1,000–$3,500 per location (https://permitplace.com/permit-expediter-cost-guide/).

## Who may file for others
- Japan: Gyoseishoshi Act art. 19 reserves preparing documents for agencies, for a fee, to administrative scriveners (https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/2842). Placetta needs a scrivener partner or the client files.
- Brazil: Law 14.282/2021 regulates despachantes (degree + council registration); not stated as exclusive (https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/lei/2021/lei-14282-28-dezembro-2021-792173-publicacaooriginal-164242-pl.html).
- NYC DOB: filers must register as Filing Representative unless owner/architect/engineer/attorney (https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/industry/obtain-a-class-1-filing-representative-registration.page).
- San Francisco: paid permit consultants register with the Ethics Commission (https://sfethics.org/guidance/permit-consultants/who-must-file-as-a-permit-consultant).
- Los Angeles: paid representatives may need lobbyist registration (LAMC 48.01).

# The 100 markets

Last updated 2026-11-01. Waves and rules: PLAYBOOK.md. The list is the top economies by size, approximately (†);
wave 4 rows are unresearched. Move a market only on the bars in PLAYBOOK.md, and log the move in its file.

Wave 1: beachheads, full investment now. Wave 2: registry and partners next, sales through referrers. Wave 3:
partner-only (a licensed local firm signs or a platform distributes). Wave 4: availability only. Source: where our
customers come from; sold from outside. Out: not served.

The pattern from the research: the portal is rarely the slow part. Local presence (resident director, secretary or
manager), apostille and translation, and capital or sector gates are. Those are Placetta's real product abroad.

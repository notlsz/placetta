## Who we beat

Contadores (and despachantes for licences): R$500–3,000 to open an LTDA, about R$249 a month after.

## The gate

Redesim has a "Meus Clientes" area for the accountant who files; despachantes are regulated (Law 14.282/2021), not exclusive.

## How a business opens here

Redesim: viability check, CNPJ, then municipal licences that vary by city.

## Who we beat

Permit expediters: $2,000–7,500 per restaurant over 6–12 weeks, $1,000–3,500 per site on rollouts, government fees on top; usually one city. Harbor Compliance (40,000+ clients) and Avalara sell license tracking, not openings, and publish no per-license price.

## The gate

Anyone may file in most places. NYC DOB filings need a registered Filing Representative unless owner, architect, engineer or attorney; San Francisco permit consultants register with the Ethics Commission; Los Angeles may treat paid representatives as lobbyists.

## How a business opens here

No national portal: state, county and city. Institute for Justice: 45 steps and 8 agencies for a typical storefront; restaurant permit fees average over $4,900 (San Francisco about $22,600). Fairfax County: median 315 days to permit a restaurant.

## Channel, payment, language

SMS and iMessage; WeChat for Chinese-owned brands; cards, Zelle, FedNow.

## Corridors

In: China, Taiwan, Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia F&B and retail brands. Out: the same brands to Canada and Mexico. Referrers: franchisors, landlords, SelectUSA, state investment offices.

## Three moves

1. Own the Chinese F&B corridor city by city: NYC, LA, SF Bay, Houston, Seattle first.
2. Register as a filing representative in NYC and as a permit consultant in SF; never be the reason a filing is refused.
3. Publish real opening times against Fairfax's 315-day median once 10 customers have opened.

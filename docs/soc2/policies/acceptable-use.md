## Accounts

1. Use company accounts for company work: the placetta.com email, and company accounts at every vendor. No customer data in personal email, personal cloud storage or personal chat.
2. Two-step sign-in everywhere it's offered, with an authenticator app or a security key. SMS only where nothing else exists.
3. A password manager holds every password; no password is reused or shared in chat or email.
4. Never share an account. Each person has their own.

## Devices

1. Full-disk encryption on (FileVault on a Mac, BitLocker on Windows).
2. Screen locks after 5 minutes or less, and when you walk away.
3. Operating system and browser updates install automatically; install them within 7 days when they don't.
4. No customer data on USB drives or other removable media.
5. A lost or stolen device is reported to security@placetta.com within 24 hours, and its sessions are signed out.

## Customer data

1. Open a customer's records only to do the work they asked for. Every opening of a saved login is in the audit trail, and staff access is reviewed every quarter.
2. Customer data stays in Placetta's systems and the vendors in [vendor-management.md](vendor-management.md). Don't copy it into spreadsheets, notes apps or documents outside them.
3. Don't paste customer data, keys or secrets into AI chat apps or coding assistants. Odelia's model vendors are covered by their API terms; a consumer chat app isn't.
4. Never ask a customer for a Social Security number or ITIN in writing. The signer writes it on the signed copy, or the agency collects it on its own site.

## AI coding assistants

AI coding assistants may read and change the code. Don't give them production secrets or customer data, and review what they write before it merges ([secure-development.md](secure-development.md)).

## Email and messages

1. Treat links and attachments with care; check the sender before acting on a request for money, a password, or access.
2. A request to change bank details, approve a filing or share a login is confirmed through a second channel before anyone acts on it.

## Reporting

Report anything that looks wrong (a phishing email, a lost device, a mistake with customer data) to security@placetta.com at once. Reporting a mistake you made is expected and never punished.

## How we prove it

1. Each person's signed acknowledgment, with the date ([personnel-security.md](personnel-security.md)).
2. A screenshot or device-management report of disk encryption, screen lock and update settings for each work device, at onboarding and in each quarterly access review.

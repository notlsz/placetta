Placetta raises $350 million led by Yeesey Capital

The funding takes Odelia, our AI agent for permits and licenses, to every city, every industry and every market a brand opens in.

Placetta has raised $350 million, led by Yeesey Capital.

A store can be finished and still sit empty. Every new location waits on permits and licenses from the city, the county, the state and federal agencies, each with its own forms, fees, inspections and renewals. The rent starts on day one. The permits come when they come. And the work does not end on opening day: inspections, renewals and rule changes follow every store for as long as it is open.

Brands that open stores for a living still run this on spreadsheets, consultants and hourly expediters, one market at a time. Odelia replaces that. She maps every requirement a location needs and links each one to the agency's own rules, fills the official forms, files them once the owner approves, follows them to approval and renews them before they lapse, in English or Chinese.

What this funding lets us do

We will use the funding to take Odelia from a store's first opening to its whole life, in every market our customers enter.

  • Every city and county. We will keep growing Odelia's library of official forms and rules until every location a brand opens is mapped from its first filing.
  • Every month after opening. Inspections, renewals, sales tax returns, fire and safety services, and every rule an agency changes.
  • Every industry. Restaurants and retail first, and now furniture, consumer goods, beauty, healthcare, logistics, manufacturing and the most regulated industries.
  • Every border. Brands entering the US from abroad, and US brands expanding overseas, on one plan for every market.
  • The largest operators. A SOC 2 audit and the controls enterprise teams expect, including single sign-on, user provisioning, roles and full audit logs.
  • Our team. Engineers and compliance experts who know the agencies our customers depend on.

What will not change

Nothing Odelia files leaves without the owner's approval, and every requirement she shows links to the official source. That will stay true as we grow.

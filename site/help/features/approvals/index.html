Approvals and filing

How an application goes from prepared to filed.

From prepared to filed

  1. The agent prepares the application and shows it for review.
  2. You check the details and select Approve.
  3. Our team submits it to the agency and records the confirmation number.
  4. You see the status change in Applications and an entry in the Audit trail.

Agency fees

Government fees are paid by you directly to each agency, through the payment link we send.

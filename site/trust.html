Your approval comes first

Odelia prepares the work. An owner of your workspace decides. Nothing is filed with an agency, paid or sent to anyone without an owner's approval in the Placetta app.

Filings In place

Only an owner can approve a filing, and our database refuses a filing request from anyone else. Before the approve button works, every line is checked again: a blank line, a placeholder left in, a Social Security number, or a form that belongs to another city's agency stops it.

Payments In place

Spending is off until an owner turns it on and sets the rules: what Odelia may ask to buy, from whom, and up to how much. An owner approves every purchase, and above an amount the owners choose, a second owner approves too. Placetta never holds or moves your money. Someone on your team pays on the agency's or seller's own page.

Messages In place

Emails Odelia drafts to an agency, a landlord or anyone else go out only after an owner approves them, from your own connected mailbox.

Requests from outside the app

If you set them up, you can also reach Odelia by text message, iMessage, WhatsApp, phone call, Slack or Lark. Whatever arrives that way, she can't file, pay or send anything from there. She puts it up for approval and sends you a link to it in the app. Team chats in WeCom, DingTalk and Microsoft Teams only receive updates; they can't give Odelia instructions.

Rolling outA message from outside the app could be a forwarded letter or a forgery, so it can't change your business's facts or name either. Odelia puts the change on a card that shows the old value, the new one, and who asked from where, and nothing changes until an owner confirms it in the app. From outside the app she also can't save to her memory, set up or stop routines, change or remove stores, or remove deadlines. A plain SMS, the easiest message to forge, can only look things up and draft.

Audit trail

In placeEvery action by Odelia, by you and your team, by our team and by agencies is recorded in your workspace's audit trail: what happened, to what, who did it and when. Everyone on your team can read it in the app. Nobody using the app can edit or delete an entry.

Rolling outOwners will be able to export the whole trail as a spreadsheet file in Settings, and each export will be recorded in the trail too.

In placeEach time a saved agency login is opened, by our filing software or by someone on our team, the opening goes into the audit trail.

Rolling outThe database itself will refuse to edit or delete any entry, from anyone, our own servers included, and will stamp each entry with its own clock instead of trusting the writer's.

Team access and controls

Roles Rolling out

Owner, admin, member and viewer roles, enforced by the database on every request. Only an owner approves filings and payments; a viewer reads and changes nothing. Every role change is recorded in the audit trail.

Single sign-on Rolling out

SAML 2.0 single sign-on, plus sign-in with Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID or another OpenID Connect provider. Verify your email domain and require SSO for everyone at it. Placetta connects your identity provider for you.

SCIM provisioning Rolling out

SCIM 2.0 provisioning for users and groups (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and other SCIM 2.0 providers), with groups mapped to roles. Deprovisioning removes access and ends the person's sessions.

How long conversations are kept Rolling out

An owner chooses how long the workspace keeps Odelia's conversations: 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or our default. After that, a finished conversation's words and your texts and calls with Odelia are deleted by the daily cleanup. Applications, filings, documents, approved memories and the audit trail stay. Each change is recorded in the audit trail.

AI assistants you connect Rolling out

Claude, ChatGPT and other assistants that support MCP can read your workspace once an owner or admin signs in and allows it, choosing the workspace and what it may read. They can only read. Each connection is listed in Settings, can be disconnected at any time, and every call is logged.

How we handle your data

Encryption In place

Everything between your browser, our servers and our database travels encrypted, over HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or newer. Your workspace, documents and audit trail are stored by Supabase in its US East region and encrypted at rest.

Passwords and keys In place

Saved agency logins, the access tokens for accounts you connect, and the links to your team chats are kept in Supabase Vault, encrypted with a key held outside the database, and only our server can read them. Our own service keys stay in server settings, never in the app or the website.

Our team's access In place

In the app, someone on our team can open customer records or a saved login only after signing in with two-step verification. You can turn on two-step sign-in, and a PIN for sensitive settings, in Settings.

Social Security numbers and ITINs

In placeOur filing software never sends a Social Security number or ITIN to an agency. The signer writes it by hand on the signed copy, or the agency collects it on its own site.

Rolling outOdelia's tools refuse to save one in an application or in your business facts, and an application that holds one can't be approved. If a document you upload shows one, it's stored as you uploaded it, so cover the number first if you can.

Deleting your data

In placeDeleting your account in Settings deletes the workspaces you own alone, with every file stored in them, except records the law requires us to keep. You can also email support@placetta.com to have any document or workspace deleted.

Rolling outOwners can delete a single document in the app, the stored file and its record together, unless a filing in progress still uses it. Each deletion leaves a line in the audit trail.

How long we keep things

Rolling outA daily cleanup removes records we no longer need: chat bot event records after 30 days, unfinished account connections after a day, records of scheduled checks after 180 days, and page view counts left untouched for 400 days. A year after an Odelia task finishes, its working notes are cleared; your request and her answer stay. Our Privacy Policy says how long everything else is kept.

Backups In progress

We don't keep database backups yet. See the roadmap below.

AI and your data

In placePlacetta doesn't use your content to train AI models, and doesn't sell it. Here is which model providers may process it, and when.

Anthropic

Claude models from Anthropic do the independent review of finished work, web research, and the work of our software that files on agency websites, and they take over Odelia's work whenever no other model is set up or one can't answer. Anthropic may see everything in a request, including your files. Its commercial terms don't allow it to train models on what we send.

Google (Gemini) Rolling out

When our paid Google account is switched on, Gemini models do most of Odelia's day-to-day work, including reading your files. We use Gemini only on Google's paid tier, whose terms say prompts aren't used to improve Google's products. Google's free tier may use them, so without the paid account, Gemini receives nothing of yours.

Backups: Groq, Cerebras and OpenRouter Rolling out

If Gemini and Anthropic both can't answer, an open model on Groq, Cerebras or OpenRouter answers instead. These backups never receive your files, only the text of the request.

OpenAI and DeepSeek Off by default

Our software can also use models from OpenAI or DeepSeek, but both stay off unless we switch them on. DeepSeek, which is based in China, would never receive your files.

Calls and spoken replies

On phone calls, Telnyx runs Odelia's voice: the speech recognition, the AI model that talks with you, and the voice you hear. Every call opens with Odelia saying she's an AI and that the call is transcribed. Placetta doesn't record call audio. In the app, Microsoft Azure turns her replies into speech when you have them read aloud.

Agent memory Off by default

If an owner or admin turns it on in Settings, Odelia keeps short notes about your business between conversations, each with its source and date. She only suggests them; an owner or admin approves each one, and what an email, upload or web page says can only become a suggestion. Memory never keeps ID numbers, card or bank numbers, or passwords, and stays inside your workspace. Your team can see, edit and delete every note, clear it all, or keep a conversation out of memory. To cut cost, the model providers above may hold the unchanging start of a request in their prompt cache for a short time (from minutes to an hour); it isn't used for training.

Email to Odelia Rolling out

You'll be able to email Odelia, or forward her an agency's letter, from the address you sign in with. Anyone can fake the sender of an email, so she acts only when the message proves where it came from: a valid DKIM signature from your email domain that covers the sender line and the whole message. We check that signature ourselves on the raw message, or use our email provider's own DMARC or DKIM verdict, never headers the sender can write. Signing keys are looked up in public DNS, through Cloudflare and Google.

If a message can't be verified, nothing starts, and you get a short note saying why. Email from an address that isn't a Placetta account gets no reply at all. Nothing is filed, paid, sent or deleted by email. Odelia answers with a link to the approval in the app.

Compliance roadmap

Placetta doesn't hold SOC 2 or any other security certification today. Here is where each piece stands.

SOC 2 Type I In preparation

We're writing down our controls and the risks they answer, starting with an inventory of every AI model we use and what it can see, ahead of an independent audit.

Daily database backups In progress

We don't keep database backups yet. Turning on daily backups is under way.

PIPL, for people in China In preparation

Our Privacy Policy already says who receives information about people in China outside China, why, and how they can use their rights. Next is a separate notice and consent in the app for people in China, as PIPL requires before personal information leaves the country.

Texting rules: TCPA and 10DLC

In placeWe text and call only a number you confirm with a code, keep a record of that consent for 5 years, stop texting when you reply STOP, and hold updates during your quiet hours.

In progressCarrier registration (10DLC) for our US texting number.

California privacy rights (CCPA) In place

You can download your workspace data and delete your account in Settings, and we answer privacy requests within 45 days. We don't sell or share personal information.

AI backup providers In progress

Written confirmation from each AI backup provider that it doesn't train on or keep what we send.

SOC 2 Type II Planned

An independent audit of how our controls hold up over months of real use, after our Type I report.

ISO/IEC 27001 Planned

An information security management system certified by an accredited body.

ISO/IEC 42001 Planned

The international standard for managing AI systems: how Odelia is built, tested, monitored and kept from training on your data.

New York SHIELD Act In progress

A written security program for the private information we hold, including Social Security numbers and agency logins: a named security owner, risk assessments, staff training, vetted vendors and regular testing.

GDPR and UK GDPR Planned

A data processing agreement with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK addendum, and a named representative in the EU and the UK.

EU AI Act Planned

Odelia tells people they are talking with an AI, and anything she writes for you is marked as AI made.

Brazil LGPD Planned

Brazil's official standard contractual clauses, unchanged, for any personal information that leaves Brazil.

Mexico personal data law Planned

A privacy notice that meets Mexico's 2025 law, and a person reviews any decision that affects you before it takes effect.

Canada: PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25 Planned

A privacy impact assessment before personal information about people in Quebec leaves the province.

Vietnam PDPL Planned

A transfer impact assessment filed before we process personal information about people in Vietnam.

India DPDP Act Planned

Notice, consent, security and breach reporting ready before the rules take effect on May 13, 2027.

Japan, South Korea and Singapore Planned

Our data processing agreement covers APPI, PIPA and PDPA, with a local representative where the law asks for one.

Nigeria, South Africa and Kenya Planned

Registration with each country's data protection regulator once we serve customers there.

Card payments (PCI DSS) In place

Card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers.

Regulated customers (NYDFS Part 500) Planned

    Report a security issue

    If you think you've found a security problem in Placetta, please tell us. Write in English or Chinese.

    1. Email security@placetta.com with what you found and the steps to reproduce it.
    2. Use only your own account and test data. Don't access, change or delete other people's information, don't disrupt the service, and don't try to trick our team or our customers.
    3. Give us reasonable time to fix it before you tell anyone else.

    We'll confirm we got your report, keep you posted until it's fixed, and credit you if you'd like. We won't take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith under these rules. Our security contact is also published at /.well-known/security.txt.

    一切以您的批准为先

    Odelia 负责准备，工作区所有者负责决定。未经所有者在快哉应用中批准，不会向任何机构提交申请，不会付款，也不会向任何人发送内容。

    申报 已上线

    只有所有者可以批准申报，数据库本身也会拒绝其他任何人提出的申报请求。批准按钮生效前，每一栏都会再核对一遍：有空栏、留有占位文字、填有社会安全号，或表格属于其他城市的机构，都无法批准。

    付款 已上线

    付款功能默认关闭，须由所有者开启并设定规则：Odelia 可以申请购买什么、向谁购买、金额上限多少。每笔购买都须由所有者批准；超过所有者设定的金额，还须另一位所有者批准。快哉从不持有或转移您的资金，由您团队中的人在机构或卖方自己的页面上付款。

    消息 已上线

    Odelia 起草给机构、房东或其他任何人的邮件，只有在所有者批准后，才会从您自己连接的邮箱发出。

    来自应用之外的请求

    如您已设置，也可以通过短信、iMessage、WhatsApp、电话、Slack 或飞书联系 Odelia。无论通过哪种方式，她都不会在那里提交、付款或发送任何内容，而是把事项提交审批，并把应用中审批页面的链接发给您。企业微信、钉钉和 Microsoft Teams 群只接收进度更新，无法向 Odelia 下达指令。

    上线中来自应用之外的消息可能是转发的信件，也可能是伪造的，因此也不能更改您企业的资料或名称。Odelia 会把更改做成一张卡片，显示原值、新值，以及由谁从何处提出；所有者在应用中确认之前，不会有任何更改。在应用之外，她也不能保存记忆、设置或停止例行任务、更改或移除门店，或删除截止日期。普通短信最容易伪造，只能用来查询和起草。

    上线中所有者将可以在“设置”中把完整的审计记录导出为表格文件，每次导出本身也会记入审计记录。

    已上线我们的申报软件或我们团队中的人每次打开已保存的机构网站登录信息，都会记入审计记录。

    我们如何处理您的数据

    加密 已上线

    您的浏览器、我们的服务器和数据库之间传输的一切内容均经过加密（HTTPS，TLS 1.2 或更高版本）。您的工作区、文件和审计记录由 Supabase 存储在其美国东部区域，并进行静态加密。

    密码与密钥 已上线

    AI 与您的数据

    已上线快哉不会用您的内容训练 AI 模型，也不会出售您的内容。以下说明哪些模型服务商可能处理您的内容，以及在什么情况下处理。

    Anthropic

    Anthropic 的 Claude 模型负责对已完成工作的独立复核、网络调研，以及我们在机构网站上代为申报的软件；在没有设置其他模型或其他模型无法回答时，也由它承担 Odelia 的工作。Anthropic 可能看到请求中的全部内容，包括您的文件。其商业条款不允许 Anthropic 用我们发送的内容训练模型。

    Google（Gemini） 上线中

    我们的 Google 付费账户开启时，Odelia 的大部分日常工作由 Gemini 模型完成，包括读取您的文件。我们只使用 Google 付费层级的 Gemini，其条款规定不会用提示内容改进 Google 的产品。Google 的免费层级可能会这样使用，因此在付费账户未开启时，Gemini 不会收到您的任何内容。

    备用模型：Groq、Cerebras 和 OpenRouter 上线中

    如果 Gemini 和 Anthropic 都无法回答，会改由 Groq、Cerebras 或 OpenRouter 上的开源模型回答。这些备用服务从不接收您的文件，只接收请求的文字内容。

    OpenAI 和 DeepSeek 默认关闭

    我们的软件也可以使用 OpenAI 或 DeepSeek 的模型，但两者都保持关闭，除非我们主动开启。DeepSeek 总部位于中国，绝不会收到您的文件。

    电话与语音朗读

    通话中，Odelia 的语音由 Telnyx 运行，包括语音识别、与您对话的 AI 模型以及您听到的声音。每次通话开始时，Odelia 都会说明她是 AI，并告知通话会被转录。快哉不录制通话音频。在应用中，当您让 Odelia 朗读回复时，由 Microsoft Azure 将回复转换为语音。

    助理记忆 默认关闭

    所有者或管理员在“设置”中开启后，Odelia 会在对话之间记住关于您企业的简短记录，每条都注明来源和日期。她只提出建议，每条都由所有者或管理员批准；邮件、上传文件或网页中的内容只能成为建议。记忆不会保存证件号、卡号、银行账号或密码，也不会离开您的工作区。您的团队可以查看、编辑和删除每一条记录，全部清空，或让某次对话不被记住。为降低成本，上述模型服务商可能会把请求中固定不变的开头部分在其提示缓存中短暂保存（几分钟到一小时），不会用于训练。

    给 Odelia 发邮件 上线中

    您将可以用登录快哉的邮箱给 Odelia 发邮件，或把机构的来信转发给她。任何人都可以伪造邮件的发件人，所以只有当邮件能证明自己的来源时，她才会处理：邮件须带有来自您邮箱域名的有效 DKIM 签名，且签名覆盖发件人一栏和整封邮件。我们会在原始邮件上自行验证签名，或采用邮件服务商自己的 DMARC 或 DKIM 验证结果，绝不依赖发件人自己能写的邮件头。签名公钥通过 Cloudflare 和 Google 的公共 DNS 查询。

    无法验证的邮件不会触发任何操作，您会收到一条简短说明。来自非快哉账户邮箱的邮件不会得到任何回复。通过邮件不会提交、付款、发送或删除任何内容，Odelia 会回复一个指向应用中审批页面的链接。

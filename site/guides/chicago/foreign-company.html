Opening in Chicago as a foreign company

What changes when the owner or parent company is based abroad. Chicago has 3 filings on this. The fixed government fees add up to about $150 and 2 filings are free.

  1. 1

    Application for Employer Identification Number (EIN)

    Internal Revenue ServiceUS federal

    Owners with an SSN or ITIN apply online. A company whose responsible party has neither (most China-based owners) faxes or mails Form SS-4, or calls the IRS international line.

    Fee
    Free
    Filed
    Paper form, faxed or mailed
    Takes
    Fax: about 4 business days. Mail: about 4 to 5 weeks. Phone: issued on the call.

    File on the agency's siteHow to fill it

  2. 2

    LLC formation

    Illinois Secretary of StateIsrael, national

    Form the LLC online, or register a company formed in another state to do business in Illinois.

    Fee
    $150
    Filed
    Filed online

    File on the agency's siteHow to fill it

  3. 3

    Create/Update Importer Identity Form

    U.S. Customs and Border ProtectionUS federal

    Sent to CBP (usually through your customs broker), or created in the ACE portal. Needed before your first entry as importer of record.

    Fee
    Free
    Filed
    Paper form, mailed
    Takes
    Up to 5 business days.
